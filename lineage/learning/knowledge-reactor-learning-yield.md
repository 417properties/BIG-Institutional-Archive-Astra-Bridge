## Causal spine

1. Founder compounding-learning / chain-reaction idea: one outcome can create multiple learning units.
2. Professor reframed “atom bomb” toward a **controlled knowledge reactor**: propagation plus governance, containment, quality control, safe-stop, useful output.
3. Reactor separated from memory/Brain and reasoning/Cortex; it transforms experience/evidence into qualified learning.
4. Founder rejected “failure” as the primary frame. Academy formalized **Learning Yield Opportunity**, **WPRP**, **Diagnostic Branch**, **Recovery Pivot**, **Negative Knowledge**, **Transfer Test**.
5. Rejected paths remain conditionally useful negative knowledge; disagreement and unsuccessful paths are not summarized away.
6. Recursive inquiry then exposed a control problem: option explosion and low marginal yield.
7. Candidate response: marginal-learning-yield / stopping judgment rather than arbitrary depth limits.
8. Later BICAS/BSIC treatment separates learning levels and adds assimilation capacity: more learning intake is not automatically more institutional learning.
