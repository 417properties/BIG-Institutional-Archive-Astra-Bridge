## Causal spine

1. Finite conversational substrates forced recurring succession while offices/work persisted.
2. Early checkpoint/conclusion transfer exposed **Succession Learning Loss**: facts could survive while causal/situational perspective did not.
3. The correction was not “load everything.” Context gravity and overload made full predecessor-context inheritance undesirable and potentially imitative.
4. BIG moved toward **Current + role/authority + major causal transitions + minimum decision-sufficient Context Delta**, with deep history retrievable only when decision-material.
5. Successful succession requires an actual representation delta and independent judgment; predecessor imitation, silent contradiction repair, and automatic authority inheritance are failure modes.
6. Present v0-facing Look From Within uses: **question → smallest relevant source → source identity/time/status → relational context → answer → stop**.
