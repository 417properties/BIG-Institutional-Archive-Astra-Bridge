## Causal spine

1. Incubation was reinterpreted from preparation for BIG into **Developmental BIG**: pre-Activation experience belongs to BIG’s causal institutional life.
2. Information loading was rejected as sufficient. Early developmental assignment became study of BIG’s own creation and changing representations.
3. Recursive Look From Within means the learner rereads historical evidence after its representation changes; same evidence can yield new interpretation, but that does not create a new occurrence or independent confirmation.
4. Historical dialogue explicitly treats “revisiting an old transcript with deeper context” as schooling.
5. Internal learning is not the terminal state. DEV3 Question 11 deliberately requires outward research after assimilation: compare BIG against the real AI frontier, identify what to consume rather than build, and search for external developments that could invalidate/commoditize/redirect internal work.
6. BICAS adds endogenous institutional-capital formation and exogenous frontier-capital absorption as interacting engines.
7. Learning/refinement/assimilation remain bounded: L1 substrate learning ≠ L2 institutional learning ≠ L3 learning-system improvement; throughput is not proof of assimilation.

## Boundary

Internal historical rereading can change representation; it cannot manufacture independent evidence. External frontier learning can recalibrate BIG; it does not automatically supersede internal causal history. Both must pass qualification/refinement/assimilation controls.
