# Archive Authority and Use Rules

1. **Historical archive ≠ Current authority.**
2. Preserve traceability: **RAW EXPORT ‒ SOURCE OBJECT ‒ DERIVED SEARCHABLE REPRESENTATION → INDEX / LINEAGE**.
3. Never allow generated indexes, summaries, normalized text, or causal lineages to masquerade as the source.
4. Preferred retrieval sequence: **question → Look From Within → smallest relevant historical retrieval → causal reconstruction → Fresh Current → frontier reasoning**.
5. Preserve causal occurrence identity: rereading/reconstruction/new interpretation does not create a new independent occurrence.
6. Consequential UNKNOWN fails closed only at its affected boundary.
7. Do not indiscriminately inject the archive into model context. Retrieve the minimum sufficient slice.
8. Redact credential-like material in searchable derivatives; do not mutate the immutable source snapshot.
9. External live evidence (GitHub, v0, providers, deployments, databases, Founder decisions) may supersede or contextualize historical transcript evidence.
