## Internal developmental turn

DEV-Δ09 records the shift from **Incubation as preparation → Developmental BIG**: pre-Activation experiences, experiments, mistakes, questions, and changing representations became part of BIG’s own causal institutional life.

DEV-Δ11 records the shift from **information accumulation → incubation self-study / recursive learning**. The first major assignment becomes study of BIG’s own creation: layered rereading, questioning, contextualization, answer formation, Founder inquiry, and rereading; reconstructing how perception changed through work/reality; distinguishing facts from understanding; and learning about the quality of its own learning.

## Outward turn

`BIG-DEV3-SX-Q11-DEVELOPMENT-OFFICE-FRONTIER-v1.0.md` deliberately separates assimilation from frontier judgment. After reconstructing inherited Development context, the successor is required to research the current external AI landscape, distinguish demonstrated capability from announcements/marketing/inference, identify what BIG should consume rather than build, and identify external developments that could commoditize, invalidate, or redirect BIG’s own work.

## Assimilation constraint

BICAS separates L1 substrate learning, L2 institutional learning, and L3 learning-system improvement. BSIC D7 adds learning-assimilation capacity and warns against conflating record/work throughput with learning/compound-learning throughput.

Thus the developmental direction is not “internal forever” or “external replaces internal.” It is recursive internal reconstruction → qualified learning → changed learner → external/frontier exposure and falsification → assimilation/refinement → changed future learning.
