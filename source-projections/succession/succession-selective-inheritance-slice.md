## Verbatim causal evidence

- **The office persists; the substrate changes.** The Development Office and its institutional state are the persistent object—not the personality of DEV3, DEV4, DEV5, etc.
- **Succession should reconstruct “where, why, and authority,” not dump history.** Current state tells the successor where the office is; role tells it where it stands; relational context explains why the office sees things the way it does.
- **Selective retrieval is intentional.** Deep history stays available, but it is pulled only when causality, authority, contradiction, experimental validity, or compression loss makes it decision-material.
- **“Loaded/understood” is not proof.** The successor has to show an actual representation delta—what changed, what connected, what remains uncertain, and what deeper provenance is truly needed.
- **Independent judgment is part of successful succession.** The successor must preserve lineage without simply copying predecessor conclusions, and the package explicitly treats predecessor imitation, silent contradiction repair, and automatic authority inheritance as failure modes.

This slice preserves the causal reason for selective inheritance; it is not a claim that every later succession implementation is proven.
