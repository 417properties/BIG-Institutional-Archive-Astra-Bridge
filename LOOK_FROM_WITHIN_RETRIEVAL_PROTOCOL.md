# Look From Within Retrieval Protocol

1. State the exact question and decision surface.
2. Identify the smallest relevant lineage/index.
3. Retrieve only the minimum thread segments needed to reconstruct causal history.
4. Distinguish source occurrence from later representation, summary, or reinterpretation.
5. Reconstruct **why** BIG reached a state, not merely what a later document says.
6. Then reconstruct Fresh Current from live authoritative sources.
7. Only after both steps move to frontier reasoning.
8. Classify output as established BIG knowledge, established practice, historical experimental finding, rediscovery, extension, open empirical question, frontier candidate, or UNKNOWN.
